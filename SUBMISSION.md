## Product Positioning

Resilience Lab should be described as reliability infrastructure for AI agents, not as a chatbot, support tool, or generic monitoring dashboard. The key phrase to repeat in the submission and video is:

> Before an AI agent reaches users, prove how it behaves when the stack breaks.

## What To Emphasize To Judges

- The product is specific to agent failure behavior.
- It directly fits TrueFoundry's resilient agents prompt.
- The prototype is interactive and repeatable.
- The exportable report makes the demo feel like a real engineering workflow.
- The launch gate turns resilience from a dashboard metric into a release decision.
